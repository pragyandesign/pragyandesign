THE CONTEXT

Fashion meets functionality

Today's wearables are more than just gadgets, they're a part of who you are. Aurawear brings together advanced technology and modern design, creating wearables that look as good as they perform.

Smart Watches Stay in sync, inside and out.
Fitness Trackers Track progress, build better habits.
Smart Rings Smarter tech. Sleeker form.
01

Research

Studied AuraWear's audience, industry and positioning to define a premium yet approachable direction for wearable technology.

  • Wearable technology
  • Tech-savvy professionals
  • Style-conscious users
  • Innovation & functionality
02

Direction

Explored a visual direction that could balance technology, fashion and simplicity without feeling overly complex.

  • Technology & fashion
  • Premium & contemporary
  • Simplicity & elegance
  • Dynamic visual language
03

System

Built a versatile identity designed to work across wearables, packaging and digital experiences.

  • Logo construction
  • Brand mark
  • Typography
  • Colour system
04

Application

Tested the identity across the touchpoints where AuraWear would meet its audience, from products to digital platforms.

  • Smartwatch & smart ring
  • Packaging
  • Digital platforms
  • Brand applications
