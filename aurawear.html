THE CONTEXT

Fashion meets functionality

Today's wearables are more than just gadgets, they're a part of who you are. Aurawear brings together advanced technology and modern design, creating wearables that look as good as they perform.

Smart Watches Stay in sync, inside and out.
Fitness Trackers Track progress, build better habits.
Smart Rings Smarter tech. Sleeker form.

The Mark

A symbol built for technology and style

The mark distills AuraWear into a simple geometric form that feels contemporary, dynamic and versatile. Its clean construction gives it the precision of wearable technology while keeping enough character to work as a fashion-forward brand mark.

Grid
Built from simple geometric forms to create a clean, recognisable symbol that can stand independently from the wordmark.
Weight
The mark uses a balanced visual weight to complement the bold AuraWear wordmark without overpowering it.
Versatility
Designed to remain recognisable across small digital spaces, wearable surfaces and larger brand applications.
