Grid
A four-point spark built from two overlapping teardrops, rotated 90° apart — distinct from the Pragyan Design icon's quartered-circle mark, so the two are never mistaken for each other at a glance.
Colour
Always the Salad → Orange gradient, oriented so Orange sits toward the top point. Never recoloured to a single flat fill.
Minimum size
16px for the icon alone. Below that, drop the "Pragyan LLM" wordmark and keep only the mark.
