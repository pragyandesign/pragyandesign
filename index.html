About Me

Pragyan Chaudhary

Product Designer

Hi, I'm Pragyan.I design products, then try to break them before you do. Someone has to.

I started in brand identity. Then a hospital website showed me that looking right was the easy half.

I found a flaw in my own design that could have hurt someone. I now ask the worst-case question first, which makes me great in design reviews and a delight at parties.

Off the clock: songs on repeat, cinematography (my filmography is gym edits so far), and Call of Duty on PC, with a win rate I'd rather not publish.
